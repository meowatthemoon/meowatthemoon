Hierarchical Decision Transformer

Advanced state-of-the-art sequence modeling in offline reinforcement learning using a hierarchical architecture.

  • Two transformers: high-level and low-level.
  • The high-level model proposes milestones states for the low-level model to reach.
  • The milestones guide it until task completion.
  • The proposed model does not require prior user interaction or task knowledge.

Decision Mamba Architectures

Further advanced the state-of-the-art sequence modeling in offline reinforcement learning by employing the Mamba architecture.

  • Two models: Decision Mamba and Hierarchical Decision Mamba.
  • Both models improve upon their transformer predecessors in terms of performance, size and inference speed.
  • Decision Mamba offers the best performing while only employing one model unlike its Hierarchical counter part.
  • Its evolutionary parameter provides the guidance signal required to outperform the transformer predecessors.

DEFENDER

Algorithm to increase the safety of RL policies.

  • Use small sets of safe and unsafe demonstrations.
  • Compare the current trajectory with each demonstration.
  • Terminate unsafe trajectories and disincentivize the agent from selecting them.
  • The algorithm massively reduces the crash rate.
  • The increase in safety results in an increase in performance.
Read the Paper →

Multi-View Contrastive Learning from Demonstrations

Framework to learn task-relevant visual representations from demonstrations.

  • Use demonstrations recorded from different view-points.
  • Train an image encoder to learn view-point invariant features.
  • Use the features in place of the images to represent environment states and train an RL policy.
  • The RL policy can now perform with images captured from different view-points.
  • The principle can be extended to other types of information such as illumination and background objects.
